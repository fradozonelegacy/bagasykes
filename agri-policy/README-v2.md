# AgriPolicy Intelligence — Indonesia

An intuitive agriculture-policy dashboard built around official public statistics, with a clean green-and-white interface.

## Proposed data coverage
- BPS fixed rice production and harvested area, 2024.
- BPS rice production by province, 2024.
- BPS Farmer Terms of Trade (NTP) and Agricultural Business Terms of Trade (NTUP), September 2026.
- Ministry of Agriculture Pusdatin, *Statistik Pertanian 2024*.

## Important data governance
This is a static prototype, not a real-time official service. Every indicator must be interpreted with its period, unit, release status, and definition. Do not combine rice in GKG (unmilled dry grain) with rice equivalent for consumption. The dashboard should clearly distinguish confirmed official data from calculated, illustrative, or pending-validation indicators.
