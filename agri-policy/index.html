Policy interpretation

What the findings suggest — not a causal claim

Research insight
Agricultural labour
The paper reports a positive association with agricultural productivity, consistent with a labour-intensive production context.
Local agricultural expenditure
The reported regression also shows a positive association, highlighting the potential role of public fiscal commitment.
Policy implication
Evaluate sustained and well-targeted agricultural spending alongside employment opportunities and farmland protection.
