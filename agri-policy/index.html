!
Data transparency noteThe study's methodology describes Cianjur Regency data for 2010–2017. Table 1 lists coefficients of 0.01 for labour and 0.04 for local government expenditure, while the narrative reports 0.018 and 0.049. These values should be reconciled against the original STATA output before any coefficient-driven simulation. This dashboard summarizes the paper and is not an official government system; statistical associations should not automatically be interpreted as causal effects.
