01 / About

Connecting theory
with real-world questions.

Research-minded teaching, evidence-led thinking, and practical insights for a changing business landscape.

As an educator and researcher, I’m interested in how economic forces and digital technologies shape business decisions, consumer behaviour, and regional development. I aim to make analytical tools useful—not just mathematically correct, but meaningful for the decisions people face.

My academic background spans economics, economic education, and marketing management. That interdisciplinary perspective informs my teaching and research across business, markets, and quantitative methods.
