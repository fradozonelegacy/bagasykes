01 / The manifesto

Make ideas matter.
Make questions louder.

Teaching with purpose. Researching with evidence. Connecting theory to decisions people make in the real world.

As an educator and researcher, I’m interested in how economic forces and digital technologies shape business decisions, consumer behaviour, and regional development. I work across business management, economics, marketing, and quantitative analysis.

My academic background spans economics, economic education, and marketing management. That interdisciplinary perspective helps me approach business questions from multiple angles—from consumer preference and competitive advantage to econometric evidence and digital business models.
